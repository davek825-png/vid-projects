The Lewis and Clark journals record nearly every mile of the journey west, yet they almost never give her a name. She was "the wife of our interpreter," sometimes just "the Indian woman."
This is the true story of how she saved the Corps of Discovery: a capsized boat in 1805, a desperate search for horses before the mountains, and a council tent where one moment of recognition changed everything.

Drawn from the expedition journals of Meriwether Lewis and William Clark (1804–1806) and the account published in 1814.
